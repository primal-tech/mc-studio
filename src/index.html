About the Studio

Michael Cunningham

For as long as I can remember, I've been an artist, drawing, painting, making, in just about any form I could get my hands on: pencil, paper, paint, clay, stone, woodwork and digital. It's the one thread that runs through my whole life, and like many artists, a constant pull of curiosity and a thirst to explore something unknown. These days, metal sculpture is a craft that feels like something maybe I should have been doing all along, one of the most natural things I've ever done, and I can't really explain why. I'll lose hours at a time in the shop, completely absorbed in a piece, to the point where time seems to stand still.

Each piece takes real time to make, and it asks something of the viewer in return: a moment to slow down and take in the intricate detail built from all those different materials. I love that process, and I love watching people's reactions the first time they see a piece in person; it resonates in a way I haven't quite felt with any other medium. I don't know what that means exactly, and I don't think it needs to mean anything. It just feels right.

That same instinct is what led me here, to a studio in Chester Springs, Pennsylvania, building sculptures inspired by nature and wildlife, where each piece starts with something I've observed outdoors and then takes shape by hand, welded from a combination of reclaimed and new steel, piece by piece, resulting in something truly one of a kind. Not cast for reproduction, not made into editions, not anything in between. One of one.

I'm always dreaming up new work, and if you're interested in something custom, that work is taken on by commission, starting with a conversation about the story you want it to hold.
