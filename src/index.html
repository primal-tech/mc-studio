About the Studio

Michael Cunningham

Based in Chester Springs, Pennsylvania, Michael Cunningham builds sculpture around the animals he notices — the hawks overhead, the owl at dusk, whatever shows up next. Each piece starts as an observation and ends as steel, built by hand, piece by piece.

The work shown here is available for purchase, and new pieces are taken on by commission — a conversation that usually starts with an animal, a place, or a memory worth holding onto.
