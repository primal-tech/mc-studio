Sentinel

Red-Shouldered Hawk

A red-shouldered hawk has nested on this property for years — you can usually spot the pair from the yard, circling the tree line or perched somewhere just out of reach. Sentinel began there, in the feeling of being watched over by something wild and steady. The name comes from that presence: always around, always alert, in a way that feels less like being hunted and more like being kept. In the wild, red-shouldered hawks favor the hardwood forests and river bottoms of the eastern U.S., holding still over open water and fields while they watch for movement below. This piece was built to hold that same stillness — so next time a hawk circles overhead, you might wonder if it's watching you the same way.
