About the Studio

Michael Cunningham

Based in Chester Springs, Pennsylvania, Mike builds sculptures inspired by nature and wildlife. Each piece starts with something observed outdoors and then takes shape by hand, welded from a combination of reclaimed and new steel, piece by piece.

The time intensive process results in works that make the viewer slow down to take in the many intricate details that are the result of the various materials used in each piece.

New work is in progress or taken on by commission, starting with a conversation about the story you want it to hold.
