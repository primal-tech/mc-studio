Barn Owl Sculpture

Locked Away

The barn owl has always been my favorite bird — an animal that feels wrapped in as much myth as biology. In the wild, they nest in barns, silos, and hollow trees at the edge of open farmland, rarely seen except after dark, which is part of what makes them feel so mythical to begin with: long associated with wisdom, but just as often with mystery, watching from somewhere just out of sight. Locked Away began as a study of that owl — gear for feather, chain for down — but the heart-shaped lock and key tucked into its chest are the real center of the piece. What's being kept safe, or hidden, or held onto is left open. I have my own answer, but I suspect it means something a little different to everyone who stops to look.
